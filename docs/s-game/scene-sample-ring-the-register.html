Design tokensEvery color is a --sg-* custom property on .sg-root — the future theme switcher swaps this one block.
Iso librarytileToScreen / prism / faceRect are the whole projection. They port 1:1 into scene/IsoScene.tsx.
Scene as dataBuildings, trees, lamps are declarative entries in SCENE, drawn in painter order — the future buildings.tsx / actors.tsx.
Timeline engineEvery frame is a pure function of loop time t — pause, scrub, and the chapter beat lists in engine/timeline.ts come free.

The full loop runs six chapters through this same renderer; only the beat list and the actors change per chapter. Reduced-motion users get stepped positions instead of travel, with captions carrying the story. No raster assets, no video — the entire cartoon is this code.
